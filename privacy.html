PRIVACY POLICY

At PLANTeeShirts, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy outlines how we handle your data when you visit or shop on our website

INFORMATION COLLECTION AND USE

We collect information strictly necessary to provide our products and services to you:

Contact & Delivery Data: Your name, shipping address, email address, and phone number used to process, print, and ship your t-shirt orders
Payment Processing: Details provided during checkout (e.g., GCash, Maya, cards, or Cash on Delivery). Payments are handled through secure, encrypted third-party gateways; we do not store raw payment details
Site Experience & Cookies: Browsing data and cookies used to keep items in your shopping cart, maintain session status, and monitor overall site performance.

DATA SHARING AND LOGISTICS

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share essential details with reliable partners required to fulfill your orders:

Courier Partners: Local delivery services (e.g., J&T Express, Lalamove, Flash Express) to deliver packages to your address.
Payment Gateways: Encrypted financial services that process payments securely.
Analytics Providers: Tools that help us evaluate website performance and user navigation.

DATA PROTECTION AND CUSTOMER’S RIGHTS

We maintain technical safeguards, such as SSL encryption, to protect your details during browsing and checkout. You retain full control over your personal data:

Access & Updates: You may request a copy of or update the personal information we hold about you.
Deletion: You may request that we remove your personal information from our active databases, subject to legal record-keeping requirements.
Communications: You can opt out of promotional emails or marketing updates at any time.

We may periodically update this policy to reflect changes in our services or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted here with an updated revision date
